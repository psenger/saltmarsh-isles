Saltmarsh Isles Help

Building

Road: 1 timber, 1 clay. It must touch your own road or building, and can't run through an opponent's building.

Settlement: 1 timber, 1 clay, 1 wool, 1 grain. It must touch your own road, and no building can sit on a corner next door.

City: 2 grain, 3 ore. It upgrades one of your settlements and gives you that settlement piece back.

You only get 15 roads, 5 settlements and 4 cities. When a button says 0 left, you're out.
