Saltmarsh Isles Help

Development cards

Buy one with the Card button, after you roll. It costs 1 wool, 1 grain, 1 ore, you draw it at random, and the game tells you what you got.

The deck holds 25 cards: 14 Knight, 5 Victory point, 2 Road crew, 2 Bumper harvest, 2 Corner the market. When it runs out, there's nothing left to buy.

To play a card, open the Cards tab and tap Play beside it.
