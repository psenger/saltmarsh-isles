Saltmarsh Isles Help

  • The goalFirst to 10 points on their own turn takes the isles.
  • Getting startedEveryone rolls to see who goes first. Ties roll again.
  • Your turnRoll the dice. Every tile showing that number pays the buildings on its corners: 1 card per settlement, 2 per city.
  • Reading the boardThe dots under a number show how often it rolls. More dots, more cards.
  • BuildingRoad: 1 timber, 1 clay. It must touch your own road or building, and can't run through an opponent's building.
  • Oh NO! A 7Nobody gets paid when a 7 is rolled.
  • TradingYou can trade on your own turn, after you roll.
  • Development cardsBuy one with the Card button, after you roll. It costs 1 wool, 1 grain, 1 ore, you draw it at random, and the game tells you what you got.
  • The cardsKnight: tap Play, tap another tile to move the robber there, then steal a card from someone with a building on it. It counts toward largest army.
  • Card restrictionsYou can play only 1 card per turn.
  • Saving gamesYour game saves itself after every move and reopens where you left off.
  • AwardsLongest road: the first connected road of 5 or more earns 2 points. Someone else has to beat it, not tie it, to take the award.
