Saltmarsh Isles Help

The cards

Knight: tap Play, tap another tile to move the robber there, then steal a card from someone with a building on it. It counts toward largest army.

Victory point: nothing to play. It's worth 1 point from the moment you draw it, and stays hidden until you win.

Road crew: tap Play, then tap 2 dashed edges for free roads. The usual road rules apply, and it ends early if you run out of road pieces or edges.

Bumper harvest: tap Play and choose 2 cards to take from the bank. The same resource twice is fine, and only what the bank can pay is offered.

Corner the market: tap Play and name a resource. Every other player hands over all of theirs.
